Know what a trade really costs before you take it.

Brokerage is only the headline. STT, exchange transaction charges, SEBI fees, GST and stamp duty quietly take a second bite — and on small accounts they decide whether a strategy survives. This calculator itemises every rupee and shows your net P&L after all of them.

Questions traders ask about trading costs

Which charges does this calculator include?

Brokerage on both orders, STT, exchange transaction charges, the SEBI turnover fee, 18% GST on brokerage plus transaction and SEBI fees, and stamp duty on the buy side. It excludes broker-specific extras like DP charges, so treat the result as an estimate.

Are the pre-filled rates current?

They were checked in November 2026: F&O STT follows the Budget 2026 revision effective 1 April 2026 (futures 0.05%, options 0.15% on premium), and NSE transaction charges date from 1 October 2024. Every rate on this page is an editable field, so if a rate changes, type the new value.

Why is my net P&L different from my broker's contract note?

Contract notes can include DP charges on delivery sells, auto square-off penalties, physical settlement costs and paisa-level rounding this tool does not model. Use this calculator to understand the cost structure, and treat the contract note as the final word.
