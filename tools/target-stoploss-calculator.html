Questions traders ask about targets and stops

What risk:reward ratio is acceptable?

A risk:reward of 1:2 or better is good — you risk one unit to make two. Around 1:1.5 is acceptable if your win rate is solid. Below 1:1.5, the math needs a better entry, not more conviction.

Should I measure risk in percent or in points?

Percent works at any price level, so a ₹50 stock and a ₹4,000 stock compare fairly. Points are fine for one instrument, but percent is what ties the trade back to your account risk.

What if my target is smaller than my stop?

Then the risk:reward is below 1:1, and the trade only makes money if you win far more often than you lose. The honest fix is usually a better entry closer to your level, not a bigger target.
