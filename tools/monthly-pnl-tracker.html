Questions about tracking your months

Why track P&L month by month instead of trade by trade?

Because the monthly zoom level hides the noise that makes daily P&L emotionally corrosive. A red Tuesday stops mattering when the month closes green — judge the system on months, not moods.

Why do I have to enter charges separately?

Because net is the only truth. Brokerage, STT and exchange fees quietly turn a green gross into a red net. Logging gross and charges apart shows exactly how much the cost of trading eats each month.

Where is my data stored?

Only in your own browser's local storage — nothing is uploaded, no account exists. Clearing your browser data clears the tracker, so keep your own record of the notes that matter.
