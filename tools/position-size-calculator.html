Questions traders ask before sizing a position

How much of my capital should I risk per trade?

Most disciplined intraday traders risk 0.5%–1% of capital per trade. The tool computes the math; the discipline is yours.

What if my stop is very close — can I buy more?

Mathematically yes, and that is the trap: a tight stop with a huge quantity gets killed by one wick beyond the stop. Respect the calculator's cap and your instrument's normal noise.

Does this work for options and futures too?

The same risk-first logic applies, but lot size and premium pricing change the math per instrument. Start with the cash/stock version here; a derivatives-specific calculator is on the roadmap.
