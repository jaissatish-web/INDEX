Questions about lumpsum projections

How is the lumpsum future value calculated?

The projection uses FV = P × (1 + r)ⁿ, where P is your one-time amount, r the assumed annual return as a decimal and n the number of years. Compounding is applied once per year, which is the standard convention for such estimates.

How is a lumpsum different from a SIP?

A lumpsum deploys your entire amount on day one, so it rides full market volatility from the start — every drawdown affects all of your money. A SIP spreads the entry across many months, averaging your purchase price. Neither is automatically better; timing of entry matters more for lumpsum investors.

Is the fixed-deposit comparison accurate?

It is illustrative only. A 6% p.a. return compounded annually is a representative fixed-deposit rate, but actual FD rates change with the interest-rate cycle and vary across banks and tenures. Check current rates before treating the contrast as a decision.
