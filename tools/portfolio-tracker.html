Questions about tracking your portfolio here

Where is my data stored?

Only in your own browser's local storage — nothing is uploaded, no account exists. Clearing your browser data clears the tracker, so keep a broker screenshot as backup.

How do I update prices?

Type the latest market price into the Market column of any row (from your broker app or NSE). Invested, current value and P&L recompute instantly.

Can I track options and mutual funds?

Anything with a quantity and a price works — lots of options, fund units, ETFs. The math is the same.
