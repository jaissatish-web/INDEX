Questions traders ask before sizing an F&O position

How is F&O position size different from stock position size?

For stocks you can buy any number of shares, but every futures or options contract trades in a fixed lot set by the exchange. Your smallest step is one whole lot, so this calculator works out the risk per lot first and then fits as many whole lots as your risk budget allows.

Why does lot size matter so much in options?

Your loss if stopped out is (entry premium minus stop premium) multiplied by the lot size, so a bigger lot multiplies both your risk and the premium you pay. Exchanges revise lot sizes from time to time, so always verify the current lot size with your broker before placing an order.

Is my data saved or sent anywhere?

No. The calculator runs entirely in your browser and your inputs stay on your own device in local storage. Nothing is sent to any server.
