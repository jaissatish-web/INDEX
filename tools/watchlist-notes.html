Questions about keeping a watchlist

Why write the reason along with the level?

Because reasons keep you honest. A support level noted as "held twice with heavy volume" can be re-checked when it is tested again; a bare number cannot. When the reason behind a level breaks, the level deserves to be removed.

What counts as a level hit — the wick or the close?

Both rules are defensible and traders argue about them forever. A wick-based rule is stricter and flags the level earlier; a close-based rule filters out fake spikes. Pick your rule before you add the level, then apply it the same way every time.

Where is my watchlist data stored?

Only in your own browser's local storage. Nothing is uploaded, no account exists, and clearing your browser data clears the watchlist — keep a screenshot if the notes matter to you.
