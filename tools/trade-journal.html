Questions about journaling your trades

Why should I journal every trade?

Because memory is a terrible record-keeper. A written log shows your real win rate, your real average R and your repeat mistakes — the patterns that turn a string of guesses into a process you can fix.

What does the R-multiple mean?

R is profit divided by planned risk: the rupee risk you accepted before entry is |entry − stop| × quantity, and an R-multiple of +2 means the trade earned twice that planned risk. Trades without a stop show a dash, because there is no planned risk to divide by.

Where does my trade data live?

Only in your own browser's local storage — nothing is uploaded and no account exists. Clearing your browser data clears the journal, so keep a CSV backup: one click exports every logged trade.
