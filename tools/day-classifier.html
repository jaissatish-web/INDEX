Tool 13 · Day Classification

Is today even yours to trade? Three checks decide.

Most intraday losses come from trading a day that should never have been traded. Answer three quick questions — momentum, trade & OI, and the gap — and get one verdict for the session: FAST, WAIT or NEVER. No data leaves your browser.

Questions traders ask before classifying a day

Is this verdict investment advice?

No. The Day Classifier is an education-only decision aid built on the 3-Check Method. It classifies the day you describe — it cannot see live data, does not know your capital or positions, and is not investment advice or a stock recommendation.

What if I am unsure about an answer to a check?

Default to WAIT. The method is deliberately conservative: if any check is unclear, treat the day as one to watch and trade only on proof. Missing a trade costs nothing; forcing one can.
