Questions about SIP projections

What return should I assume?

Equity long-term averages have historically landed between 10–14% p.a., but the market does not promise any number. The projection assumes a steady return — real markets are lumpier, which is exactly why the horizon matters more than the rate.

How is the projection computed?

Each monthly contribution compounds monthly at the assumed annual rate until the end of the horizon. The formula is FV = P × ((1+r)ⁿ − 1) / r × (1+r) with r the monthly rate and n the number of months.

Can I use this for a lumpsum instead?

Enter the lumpsum as a single monthly SIP and set years so the product matches your holding period — for exact lumpsum math, a dedicated calculator is on the roadmap.
