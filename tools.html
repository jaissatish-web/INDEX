Tool 01 · Risk

Position Size Calculator

The one calculation that separates survivors from statistics. Enter your capital, the percent of it you're willing to risk, your entry and your stop-loss — get the exact quantity and the exact rupee loss if you're wrong.

Rule of thumb: most disciplined intraday traders risk 0.5%–1% of capital per trade. The tool computes the math; the discipline is yours.

Tip: enter market price manually from your broker app or NSE. Clearing your browser data clears the portfolio — export mentally or keep a broker screenshot as backup.

Tool 03 · Patience

SIP & Compounding Calculator

The quietest edge in the market is time. Set a monthly amount, an expected annual return and a horizon — see what consistency becomes. (Equity long-term averages historically land somewhere between 10–14%; the market does not promise any number.)

Projection assumes monthly compounding of a steady return. Real markets are lumpier — that's why the horizon matters more than the rate.
