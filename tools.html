Why tools, not tips

A tool teaches. A tip guesses.

Every tool here exists because it answers a question beginners ask after losing money — and answers it before the loss happens.

Risk first

Size before entry

Professionals decide the rupee loss first and let the market decide the profit. Amateurs do the reverse. Tool 01 exists to flip that order.

Privacy always

Your data, your device

No account, no analytics on your holdings, no uploads. The portfolio tracker stores everything in your own browser and nowhere else.

Free forever

No locks, no courses

Every calculator is free with no sign-up wall. If a tool stops being free, it stops being on this desk.
